Where your GPT realtime API finally earns its place

The voice model shouldn't be the brain. It's the mouth and ears; the extractor runs 2–5s behind it; the store is the only truth. This division is what makes realtime useful instead of decorative.
🎙 Realtime voice (GPT realtime)
Fast, warm, dumb on purpose. Its session config carries:
  • the persona card
  • today's brief (~1 KB, from the store)
  • the entity lexicon — CPO, CalCard, Teresa Mahers, Marissa, DTC… fed in for transcription biasing
  • thin tools: log_status, create_commitment, schedule_checkin, paint_canvas
Rule: replies ≤ 2 sentences + at most 1 question. Anything visual → surface, and say "on your screen."
⇄utterances
context injections
🧠 Extractor (Claude, async)
Runs behind every utterance. Does the careful work the voice model must not slow down for:
  • entity writes + reconciliation against existing state
  • cross-refs ("Marissa exists — ambiguity!")
  • dependency inference (pay before the 21st?)
  • fills the clarification queue → injected back so the voice asks at the next natural pause
⇄reads
writes
🗄 The store
Entities, commitments, pipelines, expectations, preferences — every row with provenance. The Dashboard plans from it, the Canvas paints from it, the morning brief reads from it, the nag engine fires from it.
The voice session can die mid-sentence and nothing is lost — the store already has everything up to 5 seconds ago.
Why this fixes "integrated but not useful yet": a realtime model alone has nowhere to put what it hears and nothing smart behind it. Give it the lexicon (garble drops), thin tools (it can act without thinking), and the async extractor (someone smart catches what it misses) — and the same API becomes the front half of a real secretary.
